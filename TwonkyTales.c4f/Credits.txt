The Twonky Tales

Original campaign story, level layouts, localized dialogue, mechanisms and
small graphics authored for clonk-rs in 2026.

The name Twonky and the legendary bow/arrows are drawn from the existing
Dragon Rock scenario in Fantasy.c4f. Ordinary knights, bows, arrow behavior,
materials, textures and sounds are supplied at runtime by the LegacyClonk
Objects.c4d, Knights.c4d and material/sound packs. Their existing authorship
and terms remain in force; see the content repository's provenance records.
The remembering-arrow definition includes ARRW; the goal includes GOAL.

Mara, Orren, Nell, Iven, Pip, Sella, Bram and Veyr, their history, the returning
bow mechanism and this prelude to Dragon Rock are newly authored fiction.
No scripts, dialogue, film footage or graphics were imported from Twonky5.

Research: CCAN archive entry 245 and the clonkspot CR-Filmprojekt discussion
establish a separate community film tradition, not shared story continuity.
