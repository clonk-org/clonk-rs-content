A safe base with a trading unit.

Jump opens the menu for ordering supply capsules. They land somewhere nearby, unless you build a capsule port. If a capsule breaks, you have to wait five minutes for a new one.

Inside the base, the Clonk heals bit by bit.

Clonks can also be reproduced. For that, at least one Clonk, enough room, a green base and power must be present at all(!) times while breeding. If any of these conditions fails even briefly, the process fails and starts over. The process can be started and stopped with the context menu.
