Makes finished products from raw materials.

Continuous production: select an item and confirm with Special2, cancel with Dig.

Jump opens the menu of what can be built with the materials at hand. Dig shows everything that can be built, including the components required.

For the unit to produce plastic, it has to be connected to an oil tank.


Dig shows the production costs.
