# Clonk Rust content notices

This content bundle contains material under different terms. It is not all
covered by the engine's MIT licence.

Official LegacyClonk packs retain their original `COPYING` files, author
credits, and other notices. Most use Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial
4.0; Hazard uses Attribution-NonCommercial 3.0. Follow the version in each pack.
Project modifications do not remove those requirements.

- CC BY-NC 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/
- CC BY-NC 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/3.0/

The localized Clonk Party Remake scenario retains its credits and packaged
`ClonkRsLocalization.txt`, recording its source, copy/modify grant, and changes.
Its external resource packs have separate provenance.

Other imported community content is included in this local candidate under a
maintainer assumption of permission. CCAN availability is not itself a licence
grant. Preserved notices may be more restrictive, and this notice does not
override them or grant rights belonging to original authors and asset creators.

The bundle includes scope decisions in `packs.toml`, the byte-bound inventory
in `third_party/rights-inventory.json`, and the maintenance decision in
`third_party/RIGHTS.md`. Existing source and change records are in
`third_party/PROVENANCE.md`:
https://github.com/clonk-org/clonk-rs-content

selection checks. It does not change the game-resource bytes or imply that
