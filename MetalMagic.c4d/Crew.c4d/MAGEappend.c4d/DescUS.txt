Allow mages the Work with a new, fair spell system.
Now the mage has to choose his spells, like the knight has to choose his weapons. There is a Staff of Cla-Crah-Zoth, which can be transformed in one of the 4 elementary staffs. Those staffs allow mages to use 6 new, element-specific spells, when carried in the inventory. But the staffs don't use an inventory space and load the magic energy if the rule is activated.
This new system is compatible with the combo- and the normal menu system for spells: if the combo system is activated, it will open the staff specific spell menu by pressing special 1, pressing it a second time will open the normal, all round combo menu with spells which can be used from sorcerers, too. If the normal menu system is activated and you press the quick spell button it will open a menu with the 6 staff-spells. By pressing it a second time it will open the normal quick spell menu and by pressing special 2 it will open the normal spell menu of all around spells.
